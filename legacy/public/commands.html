Bot Commands

Command Access Description
!jointhebattle Everyone Allows the viewer to join in the fun by allocating them a random faction. If the viewer already has a faction, it will just remind them of the faction they are in.
!myfaction Everyone Displays the viewer's current faction allegiance, the faction standings and points total, and the viewers points and rank within the faction.
!factionstandings Everyone Shows the current faction leaderboard.
!award [@user] [points] Mods & Broadcaster Allows the streamer and mods to award points to their viewers.
Example: !award @factionfrenzybot 50
